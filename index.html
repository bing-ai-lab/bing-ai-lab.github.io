Bing Li

About me

I am a Full Professor and a National High-level Young Talent at Jilin University. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Application Technology from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT-CAS), under the supervision of Prof. Wen Gao (IEEE/ACM Fellow, and Member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering). My research interests span artificial intelligence, computer vision, multimodal learning, and generative models. I have published papers in leading journals and conferences, including T-PAMI, CVPR, ICCV, and NeurIPS.
